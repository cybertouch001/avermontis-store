Returns & refund policy

Last updated: October 2026

  • Unopened products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • Damaged, leaking or wrong items: email us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo — we will replace the item or refund it.
  • For hygiene reasons, opened or used products cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged.
  • For Cash on Delivery orders, refunds are made by bank transfer or UPI within 7 working days after we receive the return.

To start a return, email your order number to us.
