A simple 3-step daily routine

A good routine does not need many products. Most skin does well with three steps done consistently.

1. Cleanse

Wash your face morning and night with a gentle face wash and lukewarm water. Massage for about 30 seconds, rinse well and pat dry with a clean towel. Very hot water and harsh scrubbing can leave skin feeling tight.

2. Treat and moisturize

Apply a serum on clean skin if you use one, then a moisturizer. Moisturizer helps skin feel soft and comfortable — even oily skin usually likes a light, gel-type moisturizer.

3. Protect (morning)

Finish your morning routine with sunscreen. It is the easiest habit with the biggest long-term payoff.

Tips that help

  • Introduce one new product at a time and patch test it first.
  • Give a routine a few weeks before judging it.
  • Drink water, sleep well and change pillow covers often.
  • If you have a skin condition or concern, talk to a dermatologist.
