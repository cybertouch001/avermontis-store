Layering serums the easy way

Serums are light, concentrated products that go on after cleansing and before moisturizer. Here is how to use them without overthinking it.

Thinnest to thickest

Apply products from the most watery to the creamiest: serum, then moisturizer, then (in the morning) sunscreen.

A few drops is enough

2–3 drops usually cover the face and neck. Press gently into the skin and give it a minute before the next step.

Morning or night?

Many people like a Vitamin C serum in the morning under sunscreen, and other serums in the evening. Simple is fine: one serum in the morning, one at night.

Patch test first

Try a small amount behind the ear or on the inner arm for a couple of days before using a new product on your face. Stop using anything that causes irritation, and consult a dermatologist for skin concerns.
