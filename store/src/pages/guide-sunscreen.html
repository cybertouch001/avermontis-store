How to use sunscreen properly

Sunscreen only works as well as the way you use it. These habits matter more than the brand.

Use enough

A common guide is about two finger-lengths for the face and neck. Too little gives you less protection than the label suggests.

Apply it last, every morning

Make it the final step of your morning routine, about 15 minutes before you step out — even on cloudy days and when sitting near windows.

Reapply

When you are outdoors, reapply every 2–3 hours, and after sweating, swimming or wiping your face.

Don’t forget

  • Ears, neck and the back of your hands.
  • Shade, a cap and sunglasses help too.
  • Remove sunscreen properly at night with your face wash.

General care tips only, not medical advice.
