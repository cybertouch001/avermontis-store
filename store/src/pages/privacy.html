Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

We collect only what we need to deliver your order: name, mobile number, delivery address, pincode, city, state and (optionally) email.

How we use it

  • To confirm, ship and support your order.
  • We do not sell your data or share it, except with delivery partners to deliver your order.

Where it is stored

Your cart and last-used address are saved in your own browser (local storage) on your device. Orders you place are checked by our server and saved in our secure order database. If you create an account, we store your name, email and a one-way scrambled (hashed) version of your password — never the password itself. For security, every sign-in attempt to an account is recorded with its time, IP address and device type; you can see your own sign-in history in My account.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data by emailing us. Clearing your browser data removes the saved cart and address from your device.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in (it cannot be read by page scripts). This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Questions: avermontis@mail.tab.bot

Accounts, reviews, newsletter and messages

  • If you subscribe to our newsletter we store your email address only, and you can ask us to remove it any time.
  • Messages you send through the Contact form are stored so we can reply to you.
  • Reviews show only your first name.
  • Sign-in history (time, device type and IP address) is kept to protect your account.

Cookies

We use only essential cookies: one keeps you signed in. Your cart and last-used address are stored in your own browser. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
