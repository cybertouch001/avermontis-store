Frequently asked questions

Orders & payment

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, tap Checkout, fill in your delivery address and tap Place order. Your order is saved instantly and you get an order number and a GST invoice.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery is available across India. UPI and card payments are coming soon.

Do prices include GST?

Yes. All prices are inclusive of GST, and every order has a downloadable tax invoice.

How do I use a coupon code?

Enter the code in the cart or at checkout and tap Apply. The discount is checked by our server and shown before you place the order.

Do I need an account?

No, guest checkout works. With an account you can see all your orders, invoices, wishlist and sign-in history in one place.

Shipping & tracking

How much is shipping?

Free on orders of ₹499 or more; a flat ₹49 below that.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 4–8 working days depending on your pincode.

How do I track my order?

Open Track order and enter your order number with the phone number or email used at checkout.

Returns & products

Can I return a product?

Unopened products can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Damaged, leaking or wrong items are replaced or refunded — email us within 48 hours with a photo.

Are the products suitable for my skin?

Each product page lists its key ingredients and how to use it. Always patch test first, and stop use if irritation occurs. Our products are general skincare, not medicines.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page. If no email arrives, contact us and we will send you a reset link.
